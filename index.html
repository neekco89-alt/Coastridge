How We Invest

A full spectrum of capital solutions.

We structure capital around what a business actually needs — whether that's fuel for expansion, an ownership transition, or a balance sheet reset — deploying across the traditional range of private investment strategies.

01

Growth Capital

Minority or structured investments that fund expansion, acquisitions, and new capacity for companies with proven models.

02

Leveraged Buyouts (LBOs)

Control acquisitions of established, cash-generative businesses using disciplined, conservative capital structures.

03

Management Buyouts (MBOs)

Partnering with proven operators to acquire the businesses they run, aligning incentives around long-term value.

04

Minority Investments

Non-control capital for founders and family owners who want a partner and liquidity without ceding the wheel.

05

Recapitalizations

Restructuring ownership and capital to fund a partial exit, buy out a partner, or reset the balance sheet for growth.

Direct Investment

Project-level equity investments.

Beyond platform and company-level capital, we invest directly at the asset and project level — committing equity into single-site developments and infrastructure alongside our operating partners.

01

Real Estate Development

Ground-up, value-add, opportunistic, distressed, and repositioning equity for commercial, multifamily, and mixed-use projects — including SFR master planned communities, SFR build-to-rent, affordable housing, and workforce housing.

02

Power & Energy Projects

Direct equity into power generation, oil and gas exploration & production (E&P), midstream and processing assets, storage, and grid-connected energy infrastructure — spanning conventional, renewable, and hybrid project structures.

03

Infrastructure Assets

Project-level equity for essential, contracted infrastructure — transportation, water and utilities, digital and communications infrastructure, and other assets underpinned by long-duration, predictable cash flows.

04

Energy Transition

Equity capital for renewables development, battery and grid-scale storage, transmission and grid modernization, and other assets positioned for the shift to a lower-carbon energy system.

Private Credit

Flexible credit across the capital structure.

Alongside our equity strategies, we provide private credit solutions to lower middle, middle market companies and projects — structured around cash flow, collateral, and the capital needs of each situation.

01

Direct Lending & Acquisition Credit

Senior and unitranche facilities to fund buyouts, acquisitions, and organic growth for established middle market companies.

02

Special Situations & Opportunistic Credit

Bespoke, higher-return credit for complex, time-sensitive, or dislocated situations where speed and structuring expertise matter most.

03

Asset-Based & Structured Credit

Collateral-backed lending secured by receivables, inventory, equipment, and other hard assets to support working capital and growth.

04

Real Estate, Project & Infrastructure Credit

Senior and mezzanine debt for real estate developments, energy assets, and infrastructure projects across our core sectors.
